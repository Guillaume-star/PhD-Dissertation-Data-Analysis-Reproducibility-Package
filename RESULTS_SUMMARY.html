  • Post-enhancement d′: M = 4.798, SD = 0.224

RQ2: Deep learning (primary analysis)

  • Normality of the 500 paired difference scores: Shapiro-Wilk p < .001
  • Baseline d′: M = 4.390, SD = 0.243
  • Post-enhancement d′: M = 4.378, SD = 0.241
  • Mean change in d′ (baseline − enhanced): 0.012, 95% CI [-0.015, 0.038], t(499) = 0.87, p = .383
  • Baseline AUC-ROC: M = .997, SD = .002
  • Post-enhancement AUC-ROC: M = .996, SD = .002
  • Mean change in AUC-ROC: 0.00008, t(499) = 0.68, p = .499

RQ3: Difference between model families (primary analysis)

  • Normality of the 500 paired differences: Shapiro-Wilk p = .023
  • Change in d′, classical ML: M = 0.004, SD = 0.015
  • Change in d′, deep learning: M = 0.012, SD = 0.021
  • Mean difference (classical − deep learning): -0.007, 95% CI [-0.038, 0.023], t(499) = -0.48, p = .633
  • Mean difference in AUC-ROC change: -0.00004, t(499) = -0.34, p = .735
